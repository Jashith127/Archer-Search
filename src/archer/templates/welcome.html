Other Chromium browsers

Chrome, Edge, Brave and Opera. Their Add dialog has no suggestions field, so let the browser find Archer instead.

  1. Open Archer's home page once in the browser. It picks Archer up from there, with its suggestions.
  2. In Search engine → Manage search engines and site search, find Archer under Inactive shortcuts and click Activate.
  3. Open the ⋮ menu next to Archer and choose Make default. You can edit its shortcut to ar.

Don't use Add here: a hand-added Archer has no suggestions and stops the browser from picking up the real one. If you added it by hand, delete that entry and start again.
